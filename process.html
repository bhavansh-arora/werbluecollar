What do I need for the site check?

A year of electric bills, or a rough monthly average, and your plans: an EV, a battery, a pool. We bring everything else.

Is there any obligation after the site check?

No. The site check and the three-way proposal are free. If the numbers don't work for your roof, we'll tell you.

Who handles the permits and the utility?

We do. We file the permits and the interconnection paperwork, track them and chase them, so you never have to call the city or the utility.

Who does the install?

Our own crew, on a scheduled day. No subcontracted installs.

What happens after the power comes on?

You watch production in an app, and the same people and the same phone number handle questions, service, or adding a battery later.
