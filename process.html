Step 01 Free · no obligation

Site check

We look at your roof, your shade and a year of bills. You get a straight assessment of what your roof can do, even if the answer is "not much."

You

  • Share a year of electric bills

Blue Collar

Step 02 Three prices

Design & proposal

A system sized to how you actually use power, with the purchase, finance and PPA numbers side by side. The price we quote is the price you pay.

You

Blue Collar
