What goes on your roof

Seven parts. No mystery.

A solar panel is a well-built sandwich, not magic. Here is every layer, so you know exactly what you're paying for and what we're bolting to your roof.

  1. Aluminum frameKeeps it square and bolts to the racking.
  2. Tempered glassLets the light in, keeps hail and weather out.
  3. EncapsulantSeals the cells in and cushions them.
  4. Solar cellsSilicon that turns sunlight into DC power.
  5. EncapsulantA second seal under the cells.
  6. BacksheetInsulates and protects the back of the panel.
  7. Junction boxWhere the panel's wiring joins your system.
