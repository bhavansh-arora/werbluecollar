04 / Battery storage

Your solar should work after dark, too.

A home battery stores daytime solar for the evening, when power costs the most, and keeps your essentials running in an outage.

  • Daytime

    Panels power your home and charge the battery.

    Instead of sending every spare kilowatt-hour back to the grid for a low credit, you keep it.

  • Evening peak

    The battery covers the most expensive hours.

    Peak pricing runs on summer evenings, right as the sun sets. Your stored solar takes over.

    Your essentials stay on when the grid goes down.

    Fridge, lights, Wi-Fi, a medical device or a well pump: you choose what stays on.

05 / Ground mounts

When the roof isn't the spot.

Arrays on the ground for ranches, vineyards and properties where the roof is shaded, too small, or due for replacement. Ground mounts face the sun exactly right, stay easy to service and leave your roof alone.

  • Ideal for ranches, vineyards, barns and wells
  • Optimal tilt and orientation, no roof compromises
  • Trenching, permits and interconnection handled

Can I add a battery later?

Yes. Every home system we design is battery-ready, so storage can be added later without redoing the array.

My roof is shaded or due for replacement. Is solar still worth it?

Sometimes the answer is a ground mount, sometimes it's a smaller roof system, and sometimes it's "not yet." The free site check tells you which, honestly.

Do you do commercial and flat roofs?
