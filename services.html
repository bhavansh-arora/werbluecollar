Residential solar

Sized to your real bills.

Roof-mounted systems sized to your roof, your shade and a year of your bills. Under California's net billing, power you use yourself is worth more than power you export, so we design around how your household actually uses electricity.

  • Purchase, finance and PPA priced side by side

Ground mounts

When the roof isn't the spot.

Arrays on the ground for ranches, vineyards and properties where the roof is shaded, too small, or due for replacement. Ground mounts face the sun exactly right, stay easy to service and leave your roof alone.

  • Ideal for ranches, vineyards, barns and wells
  • Optimal tilt and orientation, no roof compromises
  • Trenching, permits and interconnection handled
