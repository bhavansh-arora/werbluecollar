01 / Why we started

It should feel like hiring a good contractor.

We started Blue Collar Energy because going solar had turned into a sales experience: a stranger at the door, a rushed contract, a number nobody could explain.

So we built the opposite. Every project starts with an honest look at your roof, your shade and your bills. You get three prices, buy, finance or PPA, on paper, side by side. We pull the permits, deal with the utility, install on the day we said, and answer the phone afterward.

“The name is not a slogan. It's the standard we hold ourselves to on every job.”
