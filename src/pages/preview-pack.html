First, what's working

You've got a strong base.

This isn't a rescue job. Your current site does a lot right, and we kept every bit of it, including your copy, almost word for word.

Positioning

“Solar is a trade, not a sales pitch.”

That line does more work than most installers' whole homepage. It names the fear (pushy sales) and answers it. We made it the centerpiece of the new homepage.

Choice

Three ways to pay, up front

Most installers lead with the one option that pays them best. You lead with buy, finance or PPA. That's a real differentiator, and the new site is built around it.

Proof

A real crew, a real sign, real roofs

Your team photo in front of the Blue Collar sign beats any stock image. Named leaders, a street address and a local number all say “we'll still be here.”

Clarity

A clear four-step process

Site check, proposal, install, power on. Homeowners want to know what happens next. You already tell them.

Where quotes slip away

Three options. Zero numbers.

We walked the site as a homeowner trying to choose between buying, financing and a PPA. Here's where we'd lose interest, or pick up the phone just to get a basic answer.

Compare

Payment options are described, not compared

Three cards, one sentence each. A homeowner weighing $0 down against owning outright can't see what either costs, or saves, without calling.

Qualify

No way to work out “which one is me?”

The PPA gets one line. Most homeowners don't know what a Power Purchase Agreement is, so they skip the option that might suit them best.

Capture

One generic form

Name, email, phone, message. Nothing captures bill size, property type, roof age or payment preference, so every lead needs a qualifying call before you can price it.

Value

Details before value

Visitors are asked for contact info before they've seen a single number. People still researching leave here, and research is where most solar buyers start.

Reach

Nowhere to send an ad click

Homes, business, batteries and ground mounts all share the homepage. A vineyard owner from a Google ad lands on the same page as a homeowner.

Small slip

Steps 01 and 02 are swapped

“Design & proposal” is step 01 and “Site check” is step 02, but the proposal depends on the site check. Small, but careful buyers notice. Fixed.
